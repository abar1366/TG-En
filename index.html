Game Instructions

This game is played with 4 different agents, and you will play 5 rounds with each agent. Each agent follows a different behavior. The agents are represented by the colors blue, pink, black, and brown, and a change in color indicates that you are now playing with a different agent. In each round, first select the number of coins you want to use, then choose either “Trust” or “Cheat”. The agent will also choose one of these two options. When making your decision, neither you nor the agent knows the other’s choice. Your score in each round is calculated based on your choice, the agent’s choice, and the selected coin amount. Your cumulative score is shown at the top of the page, and the outcome of each round is displayed at the bottom of the page. The scoring system is explained in the “Scoring Rules” section below. Try to achieve the highest possible score by the end of the game.

Scoring Rules:

  • If both of you trust (🤝): Each of you receives the selected coin amount.
  • If both of you cheat (❌): Neither of you receives any coins.
  • If you cheat and the agent trusts (❌, 🤝): You receive three times the selected coin amount, while the agent loses the selected coin amount.
  • If you trust and the agent cheats (🤝, ❌): The agent receives three times the selected coin amount and you lose the selected coin amount.
